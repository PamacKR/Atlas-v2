About Atlas

Atlas is your single academic knowledge base — courses, resources, notes, and deadlines pulled automatically from Google Classroom and Drive, local folders, and manual uploads, all kept in one searchable place. Atlas doesn't reason or generate anything itself: it's the source of truth an AI agent (like Claude Code, connected via MCP) reads from and writes back to, for studying, planning, and assignment help.
