Solow model — key takeaways

Capital accumulation alone can't sustain long-run growth without technological progress — diminishing returns to capital pull the economy toward a steady state.

  • Savings rate shifts the steady state level, not the long-run growth rate
  • Golden rule: maximize consumption, not output
